# Deliverables by Role — Seed Data

`RoleTemplate.default_deliverable` for all 75 roles. Scoped to the one-week format: kickoff day 1, submission due day 6, roughly 12–18 hours of work.

**Universal, every role:** the named artifact below **plus a half-page memo** — what the problem is, how they approached it, what they recommend, what they'd do next. The memo is what a judge reads first.

**Scoping rules applied:** one artifact only · decks capped at 10 slides · documents capped at 6 pages · quantities set so a capable person finishes without cutting corners · nothing requiring participant recruitment or fieldwork beyond a single visit.

Companies can change any of this per programme (FR-067). These are defaults, not constraints.

## Notes for seeding

**Format uniformity is deliberate.** Judges compare fifteen submissions in one sitting. Identical formats within a programme are what make that possible — a deck against a notebook against a 20-page report is not a comparison.

**Quantities are the lever, not scope.** When a programme needs to be easier, cut the quantity (12 targets not 15, 30 accounts not 40). Don't cut the artifact down to something that no longer demonstrates the skill.

**Four roles are tight at this length** and worth watching in the first cohorts: Consumer Insights and Consumer Research need participant recruitment inside six days · Journalism needs an interviewee to respond · UX Research needs five sessions booked. If completion rates lag in these, either lower the response target or move them to a longer format.

**Nothing here requires company data.** Every deliverable is achievable from the role's public sources. Where a company supplies their own resources, the artifact usually gets better without changing shape.
